Each team gets a dedicated sandbox inbox for checking how emails look when they are received, without configuring MX records or setting up an inbound domain. Emails sent to your sandbox address are stored and viewable in the dashboard.

<Note>
  This page covers the **inbound sandbox** for inspecting received email. For sandbox API keys (testing email sending without real delivery), see [Sandbox API Keys](/learn/api-keys/sandbox).
</Note>

## Your Sandbox Address

Every team has a unique sandbox email address in the format:

```
{hash}@sandbox.uselettr.com
```

The `{hash}` is a 12-character identifier unique to your team. Find your sandbox address on the **Sandbox** page in the dashboard, where you can copy it with one click. No DNS setup is needed.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send an Email">
    Send any email to your sandbox address from any email client or service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lettr Receives It">
    The email is received and stored in your team's sandbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View in Dashboard">
    Navigate to the **Sandbox** page to see all received emails with sender, subject, and the time they arrived.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect Details">
    Click an email to open it. The detail view has three tabs: **HTML Preview**, **Plain Text**, and **Headers**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing Sandbox Emails

The **Sandbox** page in the dashboard lists all emails received at your sandbox address. For each email, you can see:

- **From**: The sender
- **Subject**: The email subject line
- **Received**: When the email arrived

Opening an email shows its sender, recipient, and received time, plus the HTML preview, the plain text version, and the full list of headers. Attachments are not stored or shown.

## Retention

Emails are stored for 60 days. If you need to keep a message longer, copy its content before then.

## Limitations

The inbound sandbox is for inspecting email by hand, not for testing your endpoint:

- Sandbox emails are never forwarded. They don't reach your forwarding URL and don't trigger your webhooks.
- Attachments are not stored or shown
- Emails are stored for 60 days
- Not suitable for production inbound email processing

To test your forwarding URL and handler end to end, create an [inbound domain](/learn/domains/inbound-domains) on a test subdomain (for example `inbound-test.example.com`) and send mail to it.
