---
title: Schedule a campaign
openapi: POST /campaigns/{campaignId}/schedule
---

Schedules a campaign for future delivery, or reschedules an already-scheduled campaign to a new time. The campaign transitions to (or stays) `scheduled` and is dispatched automatically at the given time. Requires the `campaigns:write` scope. Not available to sandbox keys.

`scheduled_at` must be in the future and less than one year ahead. A later time returns `422` `validation_error` with "The scheduled delivery time must be within one year."

Scheduling runs the same checks as [sending now](/api-reference/campaigns/send-a-campaign-now) and returns `422` `campaign_not_sendable` when one fails. Lettr checks again when the time comes: a campaign that can no longer go out, for example because its domain lost verification or its audience emptied, is marked `failed` instead of being sent.
