---
title: Send a campaign now
openapi: POST /campaigns/{campaignId}/send
---

Immediately dispatches a draft campaign. Sending is asynchronous; the campaign transitions to `preparing`. Requires the `campaigns:write` scope. Not available to sandbox keys.

Lettr refuses a campaign that can't go out with `422` `campaign_not_sendable`, and `message` says why. The campaign must:

- be a draft with a subject, sender email and content
- send from a domain verified for your team, including the sender of each language override
- have at least one subscribed contact in its audience
- belong to a team that doesn't have more contacts than its marketing plan allows

If two send requests for the same campaign arrive at once, for example after a double click or a retry, one starts the send and the other gets the same `422`. An unexpected failure returns `500` `internal_error`.
